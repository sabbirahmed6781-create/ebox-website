Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ebox ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you visit our website or use our Android application. Please read this policy carefully.

Ebox does not ask users to create an account or provide personal information to use the application's basic features. If you contact us by email, we will receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your email address and the contents of your request.

Our website is hosted by GitHub Pages. When you visit the website, GitHub may process technical information, such as IP addresses and browser information, as part of providing and protecting its hosting service. Please refer to GitHub's Privacy Statement for more information.

2. App Preferences and Favorites

The Ebox application can save your favorite-channel preferences locally on your device. These preferences are used to provide the favorites feature. They are not intended to identify you personally. Removing application data or uninstalling the application may remove locally saved preferences.

Some content or streams accessed through Ebox may be provided by external services. When your device connects to a third-party server, that provider may receive technical information such as your IP address and connection details. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. Ebox does not control the privacy practices of independent third-party services.

The website may use technical features necessary for hosting and operation. We have not intentionally added Google AdSense advertising or Google Analytics tracking to this website at the time this policy was updated.

If we introduce advertising services such as Google AdSense in the future, this policy will be updated to explain the relevant data practices. Advertising providers may use cookies, IP addresses, browser information, or similar technologies to deliver and measure advertisements, prevent fraud, and, depending on user settings and applicable requirements, personalize ads.

You can learn more about how Google uses information on partner sites at How Google uses information and manage Google's advertising preferences at Google Ads Settings.

5. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable steps to maintain the website and application. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Information sent to us by email may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request and maintain relevant records.

6. Children's Privacy

Ebox is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through our support email, please contact us so that we can review the request.
