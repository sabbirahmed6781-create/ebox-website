About Ebox

Ebox is an Android entertainment application designed to bring different types of entertainment content together in one place.

What can you explore?

  • Live TV: Access live television streams available through the app.
  • Movies: Explore movie content made available in the application.
  • Series: Browse series content available through the app.

Our Goal

Our goal is to provide a convenient and user-friendly entertainment experience. We aim to make the application straightforward to browse and easy to use on supported Android devices.

Content and Availability

Content availability may depend on streaming sources, internet connectivity, device compatibility, and the availability of external services. Ebox does not guarantee that every stream will always be available.

Users should access content only where they have the appropriate authorization. Content owners and third-party providers may have their own terms and restrictions.
